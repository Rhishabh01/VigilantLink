# Privacy Policy for VigilantLink

**Last Updated: May 14, 2026**

At VigilantLink, we take your privacy seriously. This document outlines how we handle your data and our commitment to ensuring a secure and private browsing experience.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. URL Analysis Data
When you hover over a link or interact with the VigilantLink extension, the specific URL you are investigating is sent to our backend infrastructure for security analysis. This includes:
*   The target URL of the link.
*   Metadata about the link (if applicable for threat detection).

### B. Extension Settings
Your preferences (e.g., Theme, Enabled/Disabled sites) are stored locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`. These settings are not uploaded to our servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

*   **Security Scanning**: URLs are analyzed to detect phishing, malware, and malicious redirects.
*   **Performance Optimization**: We may cache analysis results to provide faster response times for identical URLs checked by multiple users, but these caches do not contain personal information.

## 3. Data Retention and Protection

*   **No Personal Identifiers**: We do not collect your name, email address, IP address, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
*   **No Browsing History**: VigilantLink does **not** track your overall browsing history. It only processes the specific URLs you explicitly hover over or interact with.
*   **Encryption**: All data transmitted between the browser extension and our backend is protected using industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.

## 4. Third-Party Services

VigilantLink may utilize verified security APIs (such as Google Safe Browsing) to verify the safety of URLs. In these cases, only the URL being analyzed is shared with these services.

## 5. Transparency

As an open-source project, our code is available for public audit. We encourage users to review our implementation to verify our privacy claims.

## 6. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the repository.
